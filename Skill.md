# AI Coin DNA

## Skill Overview

AI Coin DNA is an explainable AI asset intelligence Skill that builds a complete digital identity for cryptocurrencies.

Instead of analyzing only price action or technical indicators, the Skill evaluates the long-term quality, health, sustainability, and risk profile of a crypto asset through multiple AI-powered DNA Genes.

The objective is to help AI Agents understand **what kind of asset they are analyzing before making any trading or investment decision.**

# Core Strategy Logic

AI Coin DNA collects and evaluates multiple categories of information describing a cryptocurrency.

Each category is converted into a DNA Gene Score.

The combination of all DNA Genes produces a unified AI Coin DNA Profile that can be used by AI Agents as an explainable decision-support layer.

Unlike traditional market analysis, AI Coin DNA focuses on understanding the asset itself rather than predicting short-term price movement.

# Risk Notice

AI Coin DNA is an AI-powered cryptocurrency evaluation and decision-support Skill.

The DNA profile reflects available market, project, and ecosystem information at the time of analysis.

Unexpected events such as security breaches, regulatory changes, governance decisions, token unlocks, market manipulation, or macroeconomic developments may significantly alter an asset's DNA profile.

Users should always combine AI-generated insights with independent research and appropriate risk management before making investment decisions.
