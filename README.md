# AI Coin DNA

AI Coin DNA is an explainable AI asset intelligence Skill developed for the CoinW AI Trading Skill Challenge.

Instead of focusing only on price movements or technical indicators, AI Coin DNA builds a complete digital identity for every cryptocurrency by analyzing its security, tokenomics, liquidity, development, community, market behavior, and market narrative.

The result is a unified DNA profile that enables AI Agents to understand **what kind of asset they are dealing with before making any trading decision.**

---

# Why AI Coin DNA?

Most trading tools answer questions like:

- Is the price going up?
- Is RSI overbought?
- Is there a breakout?

AI Coin DNA answers a more fundamental question:

> **What is the true identity of this cryptocurrency?**

Rather than analyzing only market movements, AI Coin DNA evaluates the overall health, quality, sustainability, and maturity of a digital asset.

## Narrative Gene

Evaluates the current market narrative surrounding the project.

Analyzes:

- Positive News
- Negative News
- Ecosystem Announcements
- Partnership Activity
- Exchange Listings
- Regulatory Events
- Narrative Strength
- Public Attention

The goal is not to collect news, but to measure how current events influence the project's overall market perception.

# Explainable AI

Every DNA Gene includes a clear explanation describing how its score was calculated and why the asset received that evaluation.

This allows AI Agents to understand not only the final score, but also the reasoning behind it.

# Why It Matters

AI Coin DNA shifts the focus from short-term price action to understanding the complete identity of a cryptocurrency.

Before making investment decisions, AI Agents can evaluate:

- Is this asset secure?
- Are tokenomics sustainable?
- Is liquidity healthy?
- Is development active?
- Is the community growing?
- Does the project have real utility?
- Is the current narrative positive or negative?
- What hidden risks exist?

This creates a more intelligent foundation for AI-powered investment decisions.
